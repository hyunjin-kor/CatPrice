# Free primary-literature expansion audit — 2026-09-07

Four reaction families contain **15 candidates** (4 + 4 + 4 + 3). Their attribution, support grade, elemental/oxide mass basis, preparation route and performance conditions were audited. **40 distinct existing DOIs matched Crossref**, and actual HTTP outcomes are retained in the companion JSON. No composition, price, score, temperature input, route step or calculation formula was changed. No exact recipe was promoted from proxy status.

This is an attribution audit, not independent validation of predicted manufacturing cost or of all 116 candidates. No paid source was purchased or accessed through a purchased service. Public abstracts can establish scope without verifying a full recipe. Full papers/PDFs/SI remain local scratch and are not redistributed. Existing supplier-pack prices were not re-priced.

## Source access and reuse

The five Nature Communications full texts were readable from publisher HTTP responses. The commercial SCR case PDF was freely served by [the publisher](https://mdpi-res.com/catalysts/catalysts-09-00464/article_deploy/catalysts-09-00464.pdf?attachment=1&filename=), and the Fe small-pore review author manuscript by [OSTI](https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1726145). These are distinct from DOI-only checks. The NiMo publisher full text was readable through web retrieval while the local HTTP client received 403; both outcomes are recorded. Accessible institutional abstracts and publisher abstracts support the remaining narrow corrections.

Many publisher requests returned 403, a redirect body, or an empty HTTP 200 response. These were not counted as accessible full text. The initial encoded Crossref request for ncomms9619 returned 429; a later direct DOI-path request returned 200 with the matching title. The report records final bibliographic verification rather than calling the source missing. Publisher HTML can display institutional-access banners; no subscription-only full text was used. A public abstract was the only content used from the Balcar page.

Registered source licenses are preserved in the JSON. CC BY article licenses do not transfer to unrelated proprietary catalyst recipes or supplier data. This audit retains factual, paraphrased evidence and citations; it neither copies source figures nor grants redistribution permission for downloaded manuscripts. The existing COMET license is unchanged.

## Limits retained for the paper

Authored performance, readiness and route scores are screening judgments, not measured conversion, selectivity or lifetime. Pricing evidence confidence is separate. The audit improves source fidelity for four families; it does not claim all recipes are experimentally reproduced, all catalysts have matched test conditions, or catalogue/reagent pricing is a real industrial purchase quote. Other reaction-family primary-text gaps and unsupported grades remain open. No live collection, Comtrade request, paid API, new price, new LCA factor or framework change was made by this audit.
