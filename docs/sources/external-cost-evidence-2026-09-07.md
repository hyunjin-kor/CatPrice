# Independent catalyst cost evidence — 7 September 2026

Ten candidate cases were screened using free primary sources. The retained evidence includes three current catalog offers and one signed commercial price schedule. **None supplies all the inputs needed for a matched, independent COMET manufacturing-cost comparison.** External manufacturing accuracy therefore remains unquantified; no MAPE or average prediction error is reported. The CatCost Table 6.2 reproduction is not counted as an independent empirical observation.

The machine-readable [evidence registry](external-cost-evidence-2026-09-07.json) records sources, HTTP outcomes, Crossref metadata, observations and exclusions. The [reproducible audit](../audit/external-cost-validation-2026-09-07.json) separates catalog unit conversion from model validation. This was a bounded search, not an exhaustive global survey.

For E02–E04, USD is supported by the storefront response's non-sensitive `currency=USD` setting, rather than inferred from the dollar symbol. The selected 1 g option was checked in the rendered page. No item was added to a cart and no purchase was made. [Supplier terms](https://www.fuelcellstore.com/terms-and-conditions) characterize list prices as retail/comparative amounts and qualify their accuracy. Tax and freight inclusion was not verified. The audit's USD/kg values are mathematical normalization of those small packs, **not offers to sell a kilogram at that rate**.

## Comparability rule

A full-cost comparison needs agreement on composition and material grade; purchase quantity and production scale; currency and price date; preparation route and yield; and the cost boundary. Unknown fields fail this screen. Material cost, ex-works selling price, retail delivered price, catalyst ink and complete electrode assembly are separate boundaries. An invoice is also distinct from a public offer or price schedule.

The nominal Small Step Method production band begins at one short ton in `backend/core/constants.py`; assigning a one-gram purchase pack to a one-gram manufacturing campaign would be unsupported extrapolation. The electrode assembly model does not turn a catalyst-powder retail offer into an area-based manufacturing observation. No batch size, support grade, historical exchange rate or process was invented to force a match.

## Verification and reuse

The registry retains 23 direct HTTP attempts, including failures. Both journal DOIs were checked against Crossref and matched their titles. The DOE and Fraunhofer PDFs were retrieved and read. Direct SEC retrieval returned 403, while the same primary filing's full text was readable through web retrieval; both facts are recorded. The Springer direct response was a challenge page despite HTTP 200, so it is not labeled a successful full-text download. A superseded DOE landing page returned 404; its accessible January 2017 PDF was used instead. The blocked US Sigma product page and an EPA frame shell supplied no numerical observations.

Only bibliographic metadata, a small set of price facts, access evidence and our screening decisions are retained in the repository. Full reports, commercial pages and SI are not redistributed. Public availability is not treated as a blanket open-data license. The Hog and Mendoza Suarez papers have CC BY 4.0 metadata; commercial source rights remain with their owners. No paid database, billable API, account creation, author contact or supplier contact was used.

## Remaining evidence needed

The highest-value next observation is a free, shareable actual production or procurement record identifying a catalyst grade and composition, dry mass and campaign size, price date/currency, supply boundary and a reproducible manufacturing route. Until such a record is available, COMET can claim its published-method reproduction and software regression checks, while external manufacturing prediction error remains **not established**.
