- Electrode: chose electrocatalyst and the PEM fuel-cell CCM route, accepted the application's sourced defaults, advanced through the wizard and ran the area-based estimate. HTTP 200; `pem_fuel_cell_ccm`; positive electrode cost per cm2. [Result](screens/baseline-electrocatalyst.png).
- Reference prices: switched basis and language through the visible controls. The screen showed July 2026 for all 14 monthly-series metals and 18 review flags including four fixed anchors. Screenshot inspected visually.
- No page JavaScript errors in either complete calculation path. Early automation selector mismatches (hidden responsive navigation copies and combined accessible toggle labels) were corrected in the harness; these were not app calculation failures. No production change was required for the baseline walkthrough.
